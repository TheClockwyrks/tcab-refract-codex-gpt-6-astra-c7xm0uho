# Refract

Light follows your hand. Press an emitter, trace a path through a constellation of lenses, and watch a beam take shape on a dark optical bench. There is no clock to beat and no move limit—just the pleasure of finding a connection.

Each shape has its own color and two endpoints. Join them while threading every matching lens. Step sideways or diagonally, leave room for the other beams, and share crystals carefully: every charge must be spent, exactly once. Release to keep a path, or trace backward to rethink it.

Campaign takes you through 24 carefully arranged boards, from your first amber beam to a full spectrum of interlocking paths. Each connection opens the next puzzle, and every solved board remains available for another approach.

Cascade keeps the light moving with fresh generated boards and five rising tiers of difficulty. Settle into a quiet rhythm, untangle one more crystal, and discover the picture your next beam will make.
